# AI Voice Agent Platform

## Vision

Build the world's most extensible enterprise AI Voice Agent Platform capable of powering millions of real-time phone conversations across thousands of organizations.

The platform should enable businesses to build, deploy, monitor, and continuously improve AI voice agents for sales, support, collections, customer service, appointment scheduling, and workflow automation.

This is a long-term enterprise software product—not a prototype or demo.

---

# Product Goals

The platform must provide:

- Enterprise-grade reliability
- Enterprise-grade security
- High scalability
- Low latency
- Multi-tenancy
- Extensibility
- Cloud-native deployment
- Provider independence

---
